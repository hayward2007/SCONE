\section{\gaitname{}: SIMULTANEOUS WALKING AND ARC ROTATION}

The design goal is a single controller in which the tripod walking motion and
the continuous rotation of the distal arcs run \emph{at the same time} on the
same six joints, rather than a mode switch between a walking machine and a
driving machine. Section~\ref{sub:tripod} defines the bounded reference shared by
the walking controllers, and Sec.~\ref{sub:superpose} defines the controller that
produced the coordinated results in Sec.~\ref{sec:results}.

\subsection{Alternating-Tripod Reference}\label{sub:tripod}

Tripods $\mathcal{T}_A=\{1,4,5\}$ and $\mathcal{T}_B=\{2,3,6\}$ have offsets
$\delta_i\in\{0,1/2\}$. With cycle frequency $f$ and duty factor $D$, the leg
phase is
\begin{equation}
 \phi_i(t)=(ft+\delta_i)\bmod 1.                               \label{eq:phase}
\end{equation}
At nominal foot position $\vect{p}_{i0}=[x_i,y_i,z_i]^\top$, the planar velocity
induced by the commanded body twist and the resulting stance stroke are
\begin{equation}
 \vect{u}_i=\begin{bmatrix}v_x-\omega_zy_i\\v_y+\omega_zx_i\end{bmatrix},
 \quad \vect{s}_i=D\vect{u}_i/f.                              \label{eq:stroke}
\end{equation}
The elliptical workspace projection $\vect{s}_i\leftarrow\vect{s}_i/
\max(1,\|[s_{ix}/s_x^{\max},s_{iy}/s_y^{\max}]\|_2)$ prevents unreachable
strides. Let $S(a)=10a^3-15a^4+6a^5$ and $H(a)=16a^2(1-a)^2$. The desired contact
trajectory is
\begin{equation}
 \vect{p}_i^\star=\vect{p}_{i0}+\begin{cases}
 (\tfrac12-\phi_i/D)[\vect{s}_i^\top,0]^\top,&\phi_i<D,\\
 (S(a)-\tfrac12)[\vect{s}_i^\top,0]^\top+h_fH(a)\vect{e}_z,&\phi_i\ge D,
 \end{cases}                                                   \label{eq:foot}
\end{equation}
where $a=(\phi_i-D)/(1-D)$. Damped numerical inverse kinematics maps the six
targets to the bounded 18-joint reference $\vect{q}^{\rm ref}$. Two parameter
sets of (\ref{eq:phase})--(\ref{eq:foot}) are used and are reported here because
they are not identical: the walking condition uses
$(f,D,h_f,s_x^{\max},s_y^{\max})=(1.0\,\mathrm{Hz},0.50,25,90,70\,\mathrm{mm})$
and the rolling conditions use $(0.8\,\mathrm{Hz},0.58,20,55,45\,\mathrm{mm})$,
the smaller stroke reflecting that rotation, not stride, is expected to carry
most of the displacement.

\subsection{Superposed Rotation on Velocity-Mode Distal Joints}\label{sub:superpose}

Joints $1$--$12$ track $\vect{q}^{\rm ref}$ in position mode. The six distal
joints are switched to velocity mode, which removes the one-turn bound, and are
commanded as the sum of a continuous rolling term and the differentiated
walking reference,
\begin{equation}
 \dot q_{d,i}=\underbrace{\dot\theta_i^{\rm roll}}_{\text{transport}}
 +\lambda\underbrace{\Delta q^{\rm ref}_{d,i}/\Delta t}_{\text{walking}},
 \qquad \lambda=0.35,                                          \label{eq:superpose}
\end{equation}
which is the concrete form of ``walk and rotate at once'': neither term is gated
off by the other. Let the normalized command activity be
\begin{equation}
 \alpha(\vect{c})=\operatorname{clip}\!\left(\max_j
 \frac{|c_j|}{c_j^{\max}},0,1\right),\quad
 \vect{c}^{\max}=[0.18,0.12,0.9],                              \label{eq:activity}
\end{equation}
and let the stance/swing rate ratio be the two-level function
\begin{equation}
 \kappa_i(\phi_i)=\begin{cases}
 \kappa_{\rm st}=0.80,& i\in\text{stance},\\
 1,& i\in\text{swing}.\end{cases}                              \label{eq:kappa}
\end{equation}
With polarity $\sigma_i\in\{-1,1\}$ and alignment $A_i\in[0,1]$ obtained from a
finite-difference contact tangent $\vect{t}_i=\partial\vect{p}_{c,i}/\partial
q_{d,i}$ measured on the mesh,
\begin{equation}
 A_i=\max\!\left(0,\;\hat{\vect{t}}_i^\top
 \frac{-\vect{u}_i}{\|\vect{u}_i\|}\right),                    \label{eq:align}
\end{equation}
the unfiltered rolling rate and its first-order realization are
\begin{align}
 \omega_i^{\rm cmd}&=-\sigma_i\omega_0\alpha(\vect{c})A_i\kappa_i,
 &\omega_0&=4.20\,\mathrm{rad/s},                              \label{eq:rollcmd}\\
 \lambda_r&=1-e^{-\Delta t/\tau_r},&
 \dot\theta_i^{\rm roll}&\leftarrow\dot\theta_i^{\rm roll}
 +\lambda_r(\omega_i^{\rm cmd}-\dot\theta_i^{\rm roll}),       \label{eq:rolllpf}
\end{align}
with $\tau_r=0.10\,$s. The walking term of (\ref{eq:superpose}) is clipped to
$\pm110^\circ/$s and low-pass filtered with $\tau_b=0.04\,$s so that an inverse
kinematics branch change cannot appear as a rate spike. At start-up the three
tripod-B arcs are staggered by $60^\circ$; without this offset all six openings
can face the ground simultaneously and the body drops.

The consequence that matters for Sec.~\ref{sec:results} is that
(\ref{eq:rollcmd}) is an \emph{activity-scaled} rate, not a no-slip rate. The
commanded contact speed during stance is
\begin{equation}
 v_c=r_o\,\omega_0\,\alpha\,\kappa_{\rm st}
   =0.411\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\quad\text{at }\alpha=1,          \label{eq:contactspeed}
\end{equation}
so whenever the body advances more slowly than $v_c$ the difference must appear
as measured contact slip,
\begin{equation}
 D_{\rm slip}\approx\int_0^T\bigl|v_c-\|\vect{v}_{xy}(t)\|\bigr|\,dt.
                                                               \label{eq:slippredict}
\end{equation}
Section~\ref{sec:results} uses (\ref{eq:slippredict}) as a check rather than as a
claim.

\subsection{Stair State Sequence}

The stair controller executes Walk$\rightarrow$Drive$\rightarrow$Climb,
verifies each settled pose, braces the front middle joints, and then synchronizes
the six distal phases. A geometry-conditioned brace uses $180^\circ$, $184^\circ$,
or $195^\circ$ over increasing riser bands. This is a deterministic supervisor
that is given the stair dimensions; it performs no terrain perception and no
foothold search.
